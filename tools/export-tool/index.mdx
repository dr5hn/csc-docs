## What you can export

| Dataset | Base credits | What it holds |
|:--------|:------------:|:--------------|
| Countries | 1 | ISO codes, currency, phone code, region, timezones and more |
| States | 3 | States, provinces and regions, linked to a country |
| Counties | 3 | Counties and districts, linked to a country and to one or more states |
| Cities | 4 | Cities, linked to a country and optionally a state or county |
| Postcodes | 5 | Postal codes, linked to a country and optionally a state and a city |

Your total is the dataset credits, plus one format cost, plus any extras you choose. Extras are extra column tiers, sorting, geographic filters, translations and the flag bundle. The preview is free and its total is the price you pay.

## How an export works

<Steps>
<Step title="Select and preview">
Choose datasets, columns, filters and a format. The preview is free and shows the exact cost.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Confirming records the export in your history and takes the credits. No file exists yet.
</Step>
<Step title="Download">
The file is generated as it downloads. Downloading again never takes more credits.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Credits and downloads

- Credits never expire. See current packages on the [Credits page](https://export.countrystatecity.in/dashboard/credits).
- A paid export can be downloaded for 30 days.
- An export is pinned to the data snapshot that was active when you confirmed it. Downloads always return that snapshot, even after newer data is published.
- To get newer data, use **Renew**. It creates a new paid export for the original credit cost, uses the snapshot that is published at that moment, and starts a new 30 days.

## What the tool does not do

- It does not promise a daily refresh. New data is published only after it has been checked.
- It does not email you when an export is ready, and there is no background queue. You download from your export history.
- It does not offer team-shared credits, PDF output or an automation API.
- It does not guarantee postal coverage for every country. How well each source covers a country varies.

<Note>
The hosted API, SDKs and database packages have their own releases. This page only describes the Export Tool. Do not assume that counties and postcodes are available in the hosted API because they are in the Export Tool.
</Note>
